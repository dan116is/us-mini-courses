# Lesson 3 — Install the week: Monday plan, Wednesday check, Friday close

**Time:** 45 minutes to design; then 90 minutes of meetings per week forever (or 40 if you are solo)  
**Output:** Calendar holds + a one-page weekly scoreboard.

## The idea

SOPs die because they are not attached to a clock. A weekly operating rhythm is three short, ugly meetings that always happen. They are not stand-up theater. They answer: what did we promise, what is stuck, what cash moved.

Times below assume a US shop on a weekday calendar (Mon–Fri). Shift if your clients are restaurants with weekend peaks — keep the **shape**.

## The three holds (put them on the calendar today)

| When | Length | Name | Who |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday 9:15–9:40am local | 25 min | Plan | Everyone who touches client work |
| Wednesday 1:30–1:45pm | 15 min | Check | Same |
| Friday 3:30–3:55pm | 25 min | Close | Same |

If you are two people, you still hold them. If you are one person, they are appointments with yourself. Phone in another room.

**Do not** merge them into a 90-minute Monday. The midweek check exists to catch the lie you told on Monday.

## The one-page scoreboard

One Google Sheet, four tabs is too much. One sheet, one tab, current week at the top.

Columns:

```
Job | Client | Lane (Demand/Commit/Deliver/Collect) | Owner | Next action (verb + date) | Blocker | Cash status (deposit / invoiced / paid / overdue) | Hours this week (actual)
```

Rows: every **active** job plus every **unanswered inbound older than 1 business day**. Cap active delivery jobs with the WIP limit from Lesson 4. If a row has no next action with a date, it is not a plan; it is a wish.

At the top, four numbers you update Friday:

- **Invoiced this week ($)**
- **Collected this week ($)**
- **Overdue AR ($)**
- **Hours on sold work vs hours on admin** (rough is fine)

These numbers are for you. They are not a LinkedIn screenshot.

## Monday Plan (25 min) — script

1. **60 seconds:** Last Friday’s four numbers. No debate.
2. **10 minutes:** Walk the scoreboard top to bottom. For each row: is the next action still true? If not, rewrite it now.
3. **8 minutes:** Capacity. Each person says: “I have __ hours of already-sold work this week.” If the total is above the WIP limit, you do not take new optional work until something ships or you explicitly bump a date with the client.
4. **5 minutes:** Name the **one** SOP you will actually use this week (usually First Reply or Invoice).
5. End on time even if you are mid-sentence. Parking lot for the rest.

**Banned topics:** new service lines, new software, branding, “we should.” Capture and ignore until Friday Close has a 3-minute parking-lot slot.

## Wednesday Check (15 min) — script

Only three questions, in order:

1. What next-action dates from Monday already slipped?
2. Who is waiting on a client, and has it been more than two business days? If yes, send the nudge before you hang up.
3. Any job that will miss a client-visible date this week? If yes, the owner sends the bad-news email today, not Friday.

No new work added unless it is a true emergency (production down, legal deadline, payroll). “They asked if we can also…” is Lesson 4 / change request, not a Wednesday surprise.

## Friday Close (25 min) — script

1. Update the four numbers. Invoiced / collected / overdue / hours.
2. Move completed jobs off the board into a “Done this month” list (for retrospectives, not decoration).
3. Send any invoice that the trigger already fired for (SOP C). Do not “do invoices Sunday.”
4. Each person: one sentence — what was slower than it should have been. If the same sentence appears two Fridays in a row, that step gets an SOP rewrite next Monday.
5. Three-minute parking lot: pick **zero or one** improvement for next week. Not five.

## Solo operator version

Same holds, shorter:

- Monday 15 min: scoreboard + hours available.
- Wednesday 8 min: slipped dates + client waits.
- Friday 15 min: invoices + four numbers.

Put them after a standing cue (after school drop-off, after the first coffee). Cues beat willpower.

## Sample scoreboard row (filled)

```
Job: Q3 landing page — Northside Dental
Lane: Deliver
Owner: Maya
Next action: Send round-2 draft by Wed 3pm
Blocker: Waiting on before/after photos (asked Mon)
Cash: 50% deposit paid; remainder invoiced on final
Hours this week: 4.5
```

That row tells you what to do at Wednesday Check: if photos still missing, Maya sends the “we will slip Thursday if files aren’t in by 10am” email. The meeting does not invent the email; it forces the send.

## Done when

Three calendar holds exist for the next four weeks (Lesson 5). The scoreboard exists with every active job. You ran Monday Plan once, even if it felt awkward.

Lesson 4 stops the owner from being the invisible bottleneck.
