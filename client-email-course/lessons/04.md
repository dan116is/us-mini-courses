# Lesson 4 — Late pay, “quick question,” and bad news

**Time:** 30 minutes to save the scripts; used as needed  
**Output:** Four emails + a phone rule.

## Phone rule

Email first for money, scope, and dates. **Call** if: safety, a live event in under 24 hours, or the client has not answered two emails about a blocker you cannot work around. After a call, send a 5-line recap to the thread. If it was not written, it will be remembered wrong.

## Late pay — day 14 after due (still operational)

If Email 3 from Lesson 1 got silence:

**Subject:** `Invoice [####] overdue — need a plan by [next business day]`

```
Hi [Name],

Invoice [####] ($[X]) is 14 days past due. I need one of the following by [date, ~1 business day]:

- Confirmation it was paid (date + method), or
- A scheduled pay date this week I can put on my calendar, or
- The AP contact who actually releases this vendor.

Until it’s current I will not start new extras. I will finish [narrowly named remaining piece] already in motion through [date]. After that I pause.

I’m easy to work with on a real date. I can’t run on silence.

[You]
[Phone]
```

If they still go silent, that is collections / small claims / stopping work — talk to a human professional. This course stops at operational email. Do not send legal threats you have not cleared.

## “Got a quick question” that is actually work

```
That’s a scoped question — it needs [look at the file / 20 minutes / another round].
I can answer it as:
- part of Thursday’s update if it can wait, or
- a change (I’ll send the S/M/L price), or
- a 15-minute paid call this week at $[your call price] if you use those.

Which do you want?
```

If you offer free calls, you will live on free calls. If you do not sell calls, omit option 3.

## Bad news (you will miss a date)

Send the day you know, not the morning of.

**Subject:** `Date change — [Job] — [old day] → [new day]`

```
Hi [Name],

The [deliverable] will not be ready [old day]. New time: [new day] [time] [timezone].

Why, in one line: [contractor illness / waiting on your photos since Mon / I under-estimated the table of rates].

What stays the same: [price / remaining scope].
What I need from you: [nothing / approval of the new date by tomorrow 10am].

Sorry for the slip. The next weekly will use the new date.

[You]
```

Blame yourself or a real blocker. Never blame “the algorithm.” Never surprise them in the weekly if you already knew on Monday.

## The angry email you received

Do not reply in the first 20 minutes. Draft:

```
I read this. I want to get us back to a usable plan.

Facts as I have them:
- [scope line]
- [date]
- [money]

I can do [option A] or [option B] by [day]. If I’ve missed a fact, send it and I’ll adjust.

I’m not going to debate tone on email. I will fix the work.
```

If they are abusive, you may end the job per your contract. That is not an email template; that is a decision with a witness and a paper trail.

## Done when

The four scripts are saved. You have a personal rule: no money arguments in Slack; no same-minute replies to anger.
